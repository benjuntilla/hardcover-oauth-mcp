## What your client does

- The server acts in Hardcover as you, with the permissions you approved, whenever your MCP client asks it to.
- You're responsible for what your client, and any AI model using it, does with your account. Changes are real, and you can't undo some of them. Removing a book from your library also deletes its rating, review, and reading history. Deleting a list, goal, or journal entry is permanent.
- Grant only the permissions you need. Disconnect any time from Hardcover under **Account → API → Authorized apps**.
